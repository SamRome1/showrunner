How it works

01 · the brief gateAsk for a video and the agent hands you a brief template instead of code. Canvas, palette, assets, scenes as timed beats with exact text. It cannot be skipped by accident.
02 · the asset ruleNo logo is ever drawn by hand. Official SVGs are fetched from their source, verified on a contact sheet, and reported before a single scene is built.
03 · one grammarAnimated canvas, glass surfaces, glow that breathes, connectors with travelling pulses, kinetic type, a single warm accent. Nothing ever sits still.
04 · proof before doneThe agent renders one still per beat, reviews them, fixes what is off, then renders the MP4. CI does the same on every push.
